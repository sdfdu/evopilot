# Research Synthesis

1. Define the question, scope, and source set. Read supplied sources and retrieve missing referenced material when access and instructions allow it. Prefer primary studies or official datasets for claims about their results.
2. Build an evidence table with citations, study design, population or data, relevant measures, findings, and limitations. Mark inaccessible sources and missing values.
3. Compare results in light of differences in populations, methods, definitions, and time horizons. Separate what each study reports from your synthesis and identify unresolved disagreements or gaps.
4. Deliver the comparison and a concise, citation-backed answer. Keep links or identifiers near the claims they support and qualify conclusions to match the evidence. Use the user's citation style when specified.
