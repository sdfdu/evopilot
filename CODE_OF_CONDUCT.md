# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Welcome questions and different experience levels. Critique ideas and implementations, not people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, expose private information, or disrupt project spaces.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other public spaces representing EvoPilot.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not include secrets, private EvoPilot memory, or unrelated personal information. Project maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or restrict contributions that violate this standard.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review available context, respond proportionately, and protect the privacy of reporters where practical. Serious or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban from project spaces.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
